# Gemini-hard catalogs

The OTS catalogs narrowed to tasks where Gemini 4 Argon passes, or is likely to pass, under 80% of its runs, with Gemini-only numbers. Output: `catalog/gemini-hard-catalogs.html` (same structure as `../catalogs/ots-catalogs.html`) and `gemini_hard_universe.csv` (every selected task, its tier and its catalog example, if any).

## Rebuild

From this folder (Horizon key needed only for the fetch steps):

```bash
export HORIZON_API_KEY="$(security find-generic-password -a "$USER" -s HORIZON_API_KEY -w)"
~/code/horizon_env/bin/python3 bin/fetch_runs.py          # runs at the 2 Nov sheet's selected versions (ro3/)
~/code/horizon_env/bin/python3 bin/select5.py             # 5 Nov selection (cat/sel5.json); needs nov05/ and ro5/
~/code/horizon_env/bin/python3 bin/universe.py            # estimate, selection, samples, CSV
~/code/horizon_env/bin/python3 bin/fetch_origins.py       # runs of the tasks that unrun catalog tasks were copied from (origins/)
~/code/horizon_env/bin/python3 bin/fetch_turns.py         # turn counts of Gemini runs (turns/), new tasks only
~/code/horizon_env/bin/python3 bin/fetch_turns_glm.py     # turn counts of the GLM-5.3 Flash runs shown (turns_glm/), new tasks only
~/code/horizon_env/bin/python3 bin/fetch_files.py         # task files for samples without a local copy (show/; untrusted, never run)
~/code/horizon_env/bin/python3 bin/gh_stats.py            # cat/stats.json
~/code/horizon_env/bin/python3 bin/gh_md.py               # catalog/*.md and the CSV's example column
cd catalog && ../../pipeline/.venv/bin/python build_site.py --verify && python3 build_single.py && cd ..
~/code/horizon_env/bin/python3 bin/check_consistency.py      # recomputes every number on the pages from ro3/ and turns/
~/code/horizon_env/bin/python3 bin/check_samples.py          # each sample's heading, fields, prompt, write-up, diagram, overview row and CSV row
~/code/horizon_env/bin/python3 bin/verify_samples_live.py    # optional, needs the key: samples' pass rates and turn counts re-pulled live from Horizon
```

Data source: one snapshot. `ro3/` holds every task's runs at the version the 2 Nov sheet selects, and `turns/` holds the turn count of every completed Gemini run, both pulled at the time in `SNAPSHOT`. To refresh, delete both folders, fetch them again, and rebuild.

Write-ups for samples that are not OTS catalog examples are in `cat/samples_text.json`. A new sample needs an entry there.

## Rules

- **5 Nov selection (current, reviewer decision 5 Nov):** every task in the 5 Nov sheet's "Full catalogue" (`nov05/sheet.xlsx`, 1,781 families) whose runs at the sheet's selected task version include Gemini 4 Argon or Gemini 3.8 Flash at any pass rate, or GLM-5.3 Flash at 10% or less, leaving out tasks Gemini 4 Argon passes more than 60% of the time and tasks Gemini 3.8 Flash or GLM-5.3 Flash passes more than 80% of the time, except the 22 samples (`bin/select5.py` -> `cat/sel5.json`). Runs are the 5 Nov pull of every catalog task, all versions (`ro5/`); turn counts in `turns5/` (`bin/fetch_turns5.py`). 583 tasks qualify; 7 performance-optimization tasks fit no catalog and are left out, so the pages hold 576. Headline counts are the tasks each page holds. 168 tasks new to the report, plus known tasks outside the report's catalogs, were classified from their prompts (`prompts5/`, `bin/fetch_prompts5.py`) with the report's taxonomy (`cat/taxonomy.json`) into `cat/class5.json`. Nothing is borrowed from related tasks. The 22 samples are the ones the client has (pinned from `universe_nov02.json`, the 2 Nov selection); all 22 qualify. ProgramBench is then topped up to 283 with ProgramBench repo-upgrade tasks whose Gemini 3.8 Flash runs are recorded in Horizon under another id of the same task (the sheet's "Other task IDs in this family", or the evaluations its rollout evidence names; pulled read-only into `family5/` by `bin/fetch_family5.py`). Candidates are those where Gemini 3.8 Flash never passed (341, `cat/family_candidates.json`), most runs first; their runs count as the task's own (reviewer, 5 Nov), and the checker verifies every run id against `family5/`. They were classified from their prompts like the other new tasks, and their turn counts are in `turns5/` (`bin/fetch_turns_family.py`). The number added is 283 minus what ProgramBench holds, so it adjusts when the Argon rollout changes the base. The rules below describe the earlier selection and apply only when `cat/sel5.json` is absent.

- **Gemini-hard:** Argon pass rate under 80% (passes over graded runs).
- **Charts:** the figures are interactive, built by `bin/charts.js` and `bin/charts.css`, ported from the all-models report (`../ots_all/bin/charts.js`) with pass rate in place of average reward. `gh_md.py` embeds one JSON data block per page from `task_rows` in `cat/stats.json`. A task's value is its share of passed Gemini runs, and a model's rate pools its runs across tasks, so the charts give the same numbers as the tables. Bar-chart counts are of the selected tasks; the headline task counts are estimates.
- **Argon failure modes:** the overview's failure-mode section is the 4 Nov supplement on the 22 samples (`cat/failure_modes.json`). `gh_md.py` computes the run totals and pass@1/pass@2 from the snapshot and stops if the supplement's counts do not add up to them.
- **Models shown:** Gemini 4 Argon and Gemini 3.8 Flash, plus GLM-5.3 Flash in the pass-rate and turn figures and tables, so the figures cover every selected task. A task's GLM-5.3 Flash runs are its own, or borrowed as for Ranked tasks below; turn counts for them are in `turns_glm/` (`bin/fetch_turns_glm.py`). KPI cards and samples stay Gemini only. Gemini 3.7 Flash is left out of every page, plot, turn count and CSV column (`DROPPED` in `bin/common.py`). Its runs still count as evidence when predicting Likely tasks, so the selection is unchanged.
- **Proven:** Argon has run the task and is under 80%.
- **Likely:** no Argon runs, at least one graded run from some model, and a predicted chance of 50% or more. The prediction is the catalog's Argon share, adjusted by GLM-5.3 Flash and Gemini 3.8 Flash results on the task (naive Bayes, `bin/universe.py`).
- **Refreshed runs:** `ro3_refresh/` holds runs pulled on 5 Nov for 12 ProgramBench tasks that got new Gemini 4 Argon runs, at the same task version. They replace the snapshot's runs on the pages and decide those tasks' tiers (9 became Proven; 2 left the hard set at 83%). The estimate and the Likely predictions stay on the 2 Nov snapshot, so the headline counts do not move.
- **ProgramBench and Migration page:** every catalog task with graded runs from a shown model (Argon, Gemini 3.8 Flash or GLM-5.3 Flash), its own or borrowed from a related task (GLM-5.3 Flash and 3.8 Flash only, never Argon). That is exactly 283, the headline; it includes 13 tasks Argon passes 80% or more (tier "Argon 80%+") and leaves out the 19 tasks only Grok 4.7 or Gemini 3.7 Flash have run (reviewer decision, 5 Nov).
- **Ranked:** each catalog page holds its estimated task count (283, 123, 98 and 101). Pages short of it take catalog tasks Argon has not run, lowest GLM-5.3 Flash pass rate first, then more GLM runs. A task without GLM runs of its own borrows them from the task it was copied from (`origins/`, all versions, `bin/fetch_origins.py`) or else from the same repo's reimplementation task. System design and Debugging are filled this way; Feature implementation keeps all 108 of its Proven and Likely tasks, over its 101, and its headline says 108, so the overview total is 612 (reviewer decision, 5 Nov).
- **Attached samples:** 10 from the shortlist with Argon runs that are not main samples, Gemini-hard tasks first, then lowest Argon pass rate. Only 8 such tasks are Gemini-hard; the other 2 sit exactly at Argon 80% and are added to the System design selection anyway (tier "Argon 80%+"), in place of the 2 lowest-ranked fill tasks, so every sample is in its page's figures (reviewer decision, 5 Nov). They are written up only in the overview's samples table and `samples.csv` (every sample, main and attached, with task id and version).
- **Main samples:** `mangle-8edad8d3-blind30` is excluded by reviewer decision (`NOT_SAMPLE` in `bin/universe.py`). Up to 3 per catalog page and at least one per task group, except System design & IaC, whose samples come from System design only (`SAMPLE_GROUPS`), all in the selection and run by Argon. The strongest earlier sample (4+ Gemini runs, 25% or less) anchors each page. A task group without a sample gets its lowest-pass-rate shortlisted task, or one from the original catalog's shortlist when it has none on the current one (ProgramBench). Remaining slots go to shortlisted tasks at 50% or less, lowest Gemini pass rate first (`bin/universe.py`).
- **Catalog merges:** for presentation only, three task groups move into other catalogs. Migration goes to ProgramBench as its own domain, "Cross-language migration", and Infrastructure as code goes to System design as "State-preserving infrastructure changes". Data engineering goes to Feature implementation inside the existing "Data engineering & databases" domain, keeping its sub-domains, so the page has no second data-pipeline domain (`MERGE` in `bin/gh_stats.py`). Selection and estimates still use each group's own Argon share.
