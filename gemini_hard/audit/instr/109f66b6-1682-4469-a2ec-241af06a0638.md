Kitex cannot currently provide structured, concurrency-safe observation of every actual RPC attempt in a logical unary call. Implement this for sequential failure retries, concurrent backups, mixed retries, and calls without retries. Keep the retry API at `github.com/cloudwego/kitex/pkg/retry`, `WithRetryAttemptObserver` at `github.com/cloudwego/kitex/client`, and `RetryAttemptStart` and `RetryAttemptFinish` at `github.com/cloudwego/kitex/pkg/stats`.

Give each call a distinct identity and each execution exactly one consecutive positive sequence number shared by its records. Report every execution from start through completion in causal order, then exactly one summary distinguishing success, failure, and rejection.

Isolate observer delivery so slow or panicking observers cannot affect other observers, execution, retry decisions, or the returned result. Report result-driven and timer-driven launches, backoff and stopping decisions, exhaustion, overlapping completions, and the selected execution accurately. Direct, disabled, limited, rejected, and early-completing paths must report only executions that occurred.

Use immutable snapshots. Identity must survive RPCInfo copying, pooled-state reuse must not rewrite records or leak identity between calls, and statistics and circuit-break accounting must correspond exactly once to the same executions and identities. Preserve response selection, errors, fallback, middleware visibility, retry metadata, and existing client behavior.

Matching observer and statistics sequences must cover three-execution failure retries. Completions follow starts. A later-started winning backup remains selected after losing completions and RPCInfo recycling. Observer panics during exhaustion cannot block termination delivery to another observer or replace the original RPC error. Mixed timer-driven and result-driven launches must produce identical execution sets in observations, statistics, and circuit-break accounting. Direct calls, stopping conditions, fallback after exhaustion, and concurrent backup copies retain their behavior and stable, distinct correlation.
