Ariadne cannot currently classify failures consistently across its HTTP, WSGI, graphql-ws, graphql-transport-ws, and SSE transports. Add one configurable synchronous or asynchronous `ErrorPolicy` that receives transport-neutral context and selects a legal outcome for each transport. Preserve existing behavior without a policy.

Classify every covered failure exactly once with `FailureObservation`, preserving the original exception, accurate `ErrorPhase` and `TransportKind`, genuinely available request and operation context, and operation identity for subscription failures. Keep parsing, validation, execution, subscription startup, subscription iteration, and terminal subscription failures distinct. Ordinary GraphQL errors retain the configured formatter and Ariadne `GraphQLResult` semantics.

`GraphQLHTTPHandler` and `WSGIGraphQL` must translate `HTTPErrorOutcome` deterministically without changing successful status, headers, or data behavior. Policy failures never re-enter classification and produce deterministic, protocol-safe fallbacks.
