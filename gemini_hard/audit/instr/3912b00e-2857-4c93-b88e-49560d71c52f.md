gmqtt cannot gracefully remove individual or all broker listeners without disrupting admitted work. Implement bounded, transport-aware draining through the broker and management HTTP for TCP, Net, Unix socket, QUIC, WebSocket, HTTP, and HTTP health-check listeners.

Listeners from NewTCP, NewNet, NewUnixSock, NewQUIC, NewWebsocket, NewHTTP, and NewHTTPHealthCheck must have one atomic admission boundary, count native work units, and drain through monotonic, retry-safe states. Racing work must enter Server.EstablishConnection and be counted exactly once, or be rejected and fully closed without an owner gap.

Deadlines report timed-out state and outstanding work without closing clients. Forced completion closes only clients owned by targeted listeners; Close keeps its destructive semantics. A timed-out WebSocket stays usable until a forced retry targets it. Repeated drains avoid duplicate shutdown effects, and ordinary Serve then Close remains unchanged.

Named draining must not hold collection coordination while finishing, so other lookups remain responsive. Unknown IDs leave the registry unchanged and are reported distinctly. Aggregate draining preserves partial successes and sorts reports by listener ID. Mixed TCP and QUIC drains preserve stream counts. Across listener types and retries, Server.DrainListeners and REST must identically report completed, timed-out, already-drained, and unknown-listener outcomes, preserving identities, protocols, counts, DrainError classifications, and deterministic order. Equivalent all-listener requests return matching ordered reports. After mixed timeout, a forced retry closes only targeted clients and REST exposes matching remaining counts.

The listeners package exports DrainState, DrainOptions, DrainReport, DrainErrorKind, DrainError, Listener.Drain, Listeners.Drain, and Listeners.DrainAll; mqtt exports DrainRequest and Server.DrainListeners. The rest package exposes MqttDrainListenersPath as exactly "/api/v1/mqtt/listeners/drain". Rest.GenHandlers accepts POST draining and distinguishes malformed requests from unknown listeners. Without draining, existing Serve, metadata, connection establishment, health checks, and Close behavior stays unchanged.
