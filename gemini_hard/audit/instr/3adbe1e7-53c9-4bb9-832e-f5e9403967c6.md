## Goal

Fix PayPal expense payouts in `/app`: the payout cron can submit the same scheduled approval twice after a lost response, payout item webhooks do not settle expenses, and a returned payout can leave the collective debited. The web process, hourly payout job (`cron/hourly/10-pay-paypal-scheduled-expenses-payouts.js`) and hourly status job (`cron/hourly/11-check-pending-paypal-expenses.js`) must agree on each expense's payment and ledger state.

## Requirements

1. A scheduled PayPal approval produces at most one accepted payout item. Group eligible expenses of the same host and currency in a batch. Persist sufficient identity before submitting a batch so that killing the payout process after PayPal accepts it, then rerunning either job, recovers that payout without another accepted item. A genuinely failed attempt enters `ERROR`; `SCHEDULE_FOR_PAYMENT` can then reschedule it for submission with a fresh identity.
2. `processExpense` on GraphQL v2 continues to schedule and unschedule an expense before submission; `UNSCHEDULE_PAYMENT`, `PAY` with `forceManual`, and `MARK_AS_UNPAID` cannot produce a manual payment or erase an in-flight PayPal payment after submission may have reached PayPal.
3. `POST /webhooks/paypal/:hostId` processes verified `PAYMENT.PAYOUTS-ITEM.SUCCEEDED`, `FAILED`, `BLOCKED`, `RETURNED`, `REFUNDED`, and `REVERSED` events. Webhooks and the status job converge on one result even when they run together or events are duplicated or reordered. Success sets the expense to `PAID` and creates one expense debit and one paid activity; failure or blocking before success sets it to `ERROR` with no debit. A return, refund or reversal after success compensates the ledger exactly once with a refund credit, restores the pre-payout balance and sets the expense to `ERROR` so it can be scheduled again; a stale success cannot undo that reversal. A later approved attempt may be paid once.
4. Continue settling expenses already `PROCESSING` with `data.payout_batch_id` and recently `PAID` with that field, even when they predate the new claim mechanism.

## Keep compatible

Keep the GraphQL v2 `processExpense` actions `SCHEDULE_FOR_PAYMENT`, `UNSCHEDULE_PAYMENT`, `PAY`, and `MARK_AS_UNPAID`, including manual payments for expenses without an in-flight PayPal payout when a positive `totalAmountPaidInHostCurrency` is supplied. Keep the PayPal webhook route and payout batch polling interface.

## Environment

The repository is at `/app`. PostgreSQL 16 and Redis run locally (`sudo service postgresql start`, `sudo service redis-server start`). The database is `opencollective_test`, with `PG_URL=postgres://app:app@localhost:5432/opencollective_test`; `/opt/sd-seed.sql` contains development fixtures. Dependencies are installed and the server builds offline with `npm run build:server`. PayPal sandbox traffic is served through a local TLS fixture at `api.sandbox.paypal.com` with `NODE_EXTRA_CA_CERTS=/opt/sd-cert.pem` in verification; do not require live PayPal access.
