A room's agent dispatch can outlive its worker: the worker connection disappears, but the room still reports its job as running and never assigns a replacement. This also happens when the server holding the worker connection goes away while the room remains on another node.

To close this out:
- For room jobs (`JT_ROOM`) started through `CreateDispatch` or room creation, assign an available worker within 10 seconds. Show the initial attempt in `ListDispatch` within 3 seconds of assignment; keep every attempt there with its current worker-reported status within 2 seconds of `UpdateJobStatus`, whether the API request reaches the room's node or another node.
- A closed worker socket triggers a replacement on an available worker within 15 seconds; show the lost attempt as failed and the new attempt as running. If the worker's server node is killed or stopped, recover onto a surviving node within 20 seconds. A worker that stops responding to pings for more than 10 seconds is also lost and replaced within 15 seconds.
- Never run two attempts for the same room dispatch simultaneously. A superseded worker that resumes receives `JobTermination` within 5 seconds; its subsequent status updates cannot change dispatch state, and the old attempt's agent identity cannot rejoin the room. Remove an already-joined superseded agent within 5 seconds of replacement.
- `DeleteDispatch` and `DeleteRoom` send termination to their running workers within 5 seconds and preclude further attempts, including when a worker fails concurrently with deletion or immediately afterward. Once deletion returns, no replacement is assigned.

Keep existing worker registration, assignment and termination messages, room creation, dispatch listing, and participant joins functional. Publisher and participant jobs are outside this change. The application lives in `/app`; build with `go build ./...` from `/app`. Redis is available locally (`sudo service redis-server start`); multiple server processes may share it. Docker is unavailable inside the solver container.
