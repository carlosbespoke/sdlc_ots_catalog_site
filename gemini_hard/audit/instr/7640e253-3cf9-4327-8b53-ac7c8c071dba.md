We have a design flaw in our time handling. Right now a single `ITimeProvider` (along with direct `DateTime.UtcNow` / `DateTime.Now` calls) is being used both for security token/replay validation (which must ALWAYS use strict machine UTC) and for business shift, attendance, and overtime display bucketing rules (which must use the tenant's configured business timezone).

Split this cleanly into `IAntiReplayClock` (`AntiReplayClock`) and `IShiftRuleClock` (`ShiftRuleClock`) across the Application and Infrastructure layers, wire both in the DI container, and eliminate all direct `DateTime.UtcNow` and `DateTime.Now` calls across Domain, Application, and overtime formatting helpers. All 68 existing unit tests (`dotnet test`) must pass, and add unit tests proving that a `UTC+3` business timezone shifts daily overtime rollover at `21:00 UTC` (`00:00 UTC+3`) without affecting `IAntiReplayClock` token validation.
