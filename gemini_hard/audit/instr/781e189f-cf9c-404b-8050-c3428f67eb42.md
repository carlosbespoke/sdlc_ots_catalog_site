go-streams cannot persist restart-safe source progress or resume without silently skipping work. Implement opt-in portable checkpoints that deterministically encode each source's identity, generation, native position kind and bytes, bound to its configuration, while leaving ordinary channel pipelines unchanged.

Reject corrupt tokens and incompatible configurations. Stores expose only complete monotonic saves. Persistence failures and conflicts remain visible without replacing the last durable position. Sources commit only a contiguous safe frontier; later completion cannot cross unfinished earlier work.

FileSource and ReaderSource resume at parser-confirmed boundaries. ReaderSource is accurate when seekable and otherwise reports unsupported without pre-reading. ChanSource remains the supplied channel and reports unsupported without reading it. A stored mid-file checkpoint resumes in a new source with only remaining complete lines. Parser lookahead followed by failed persistence leaves uncommitted bytes available after restoration.

Broker, object-storage, and polling connectors are outside the scope of this checkpoint implementation.

Unconfigured constructors, values, Via composition through Map, Batch, and SlidingWindow, cancellation, closure, and completion retain existing behavior.
