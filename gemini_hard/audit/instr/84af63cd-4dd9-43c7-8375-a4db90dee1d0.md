Give Owncast one coherent, observable lifecycle for its long-running services so startup, shutdown, recovery, and operator status agree. Preserve established behavior while making partial failure, drain progress, and restart safety visible end to end.

Immutable snapshots distinguish every lifecycle state, preserve ordered receipts, and converge with the stable aggregate shutdown report only after all started work terminates. Startup follows successful dependencies in registration order; draining reverses dependency and registration order, isolates failures, and waits for terminal outcomes. Concurrent or repeated requests are idempotent, and restart is allowed only after complete resource release. Across plugins, ActivityPub delivery, stale-server checking, transcoding, thumbnails, directory registration, and StreamEngine, each supervised participant reports completion only after its own active work and resource-release boundary is terminal: draining consistently rejects new work, settles admitted calls and claims, separately confirms transcoder child-process termination and thumbnail artifact-loop completion, silences heartbeat and ticker callbacks, preserves interrupted durable work for recovery, and retains release or final-update failures alongside earlier outcomes; StreamEngine preserves inbound connection handling and forces broadcaster disconnection before shutdown completes. Disabled, idle, and repeated-stop behavior remains compatible. Authenticated status and its typed admin consumer preserve deterministic ordering, aggregate flags, states, receipts, and every structured failure without null collections or semantic loss.
