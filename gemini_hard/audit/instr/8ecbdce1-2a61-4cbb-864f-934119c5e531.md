# Booking seat holds

Today retries, races and crashes can share or strand seats and lose booking events. Fix Booking's create-booking flow in /app.

Booking:
- `POST /api/v1/booking` takes an `Idempotency-Key` header and returns the booking ID as `id`. One key gives one booking and one seat, even concurrently or across Booking instances.
- New authenticated `GET /api/v1/booking/{id}` returns `id`, `status: "Confirmed"` and `seatNumber` as soon as the POST succeeds.
- Twelve distinct requests for a five-seat flight: exactly five confirm, on distinct seats; the rest fail.
- Unknown or full flights fail without claiming a seat. A request cut short by a Flight outage or Booking crash takes no seat and leaves the key retryable after recovery.
- `BookingCreated` carries the booking ID and is published once per confirmed booking within 20 seconds, never for a failed attempt. Booking confirms bookings even while it cannot reach RabbitMQ. If Booking crashes after confirming but before the event reaches RabbitMQ, it is published exactly once within two minutes of the crash, once Booking is restarted.

Flight:
- In both `flight.proto` copies, additively: `BookingId` on `ReserveSeatRequest`, `SeatNumber` on `ReserveSeatResult` (beside its Id), and `ConfirmSeat` and `ReleaseSeat`, each taking `HoldRequest { BookingId }` and returning `ReserveSeatResult`.
- Holds are atomic per booking ID: a retry gets the same result; a competing ID gets an empty `SeatNumber` or `ResourceExhausted`.
- Held and confirmed seats leave `GET /api/v1/flight/get-available-seats/{id}` and the gRPC availability list (confirmed ones within five seconds); a full flight lists none. Unconfirmed holds expire within 45 seconds of acquisition (nominal lease 30 seconds), even if Booking dies; released holds reappear within five seconds; confirmed seats never expire.
- Keep that route's seat fields and the existing gRPC flight and seat response fields.

Setup: containers `postgres`, `mongo`, `rabbitmq` and `eventstore` are running; services reach RabbitMQ through `RabbitMqOptions` (host and port). `run-booking-stack` builds /app and starts Identity, Flight, Passenger and Booking as verification does and prints their URLs (logs in /tmp/booking-stack; `stop` stops them).
