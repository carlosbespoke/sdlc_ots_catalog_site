Give Arcade MCP servers a protocol-aware response byte limit that produces valid, deterministic outcomes instead of truncating serialized data. Apply one UTF-8 accounting contract from middleware and dispatch through HTTP JSON, SSE, and stdio while preserving unlimited deployments.

`ResponseSizer.measure` counts the exact alias-preserving transport JSON bytes without mutation, including transport null-exclusion rules, while stdio framing remains outside that count. `ResponseBudget.remaining(self)` validates positive limits, reserves envelope bytes, and never reports a negative allowance; invalid limits raise `ResponseLimitConfigurationError`. Per-request budgets travel in `context.metadata["response_budget"]`. `ResponseReducer.reduce` either returns a schema-valid, type-preserving `ReductionResult` within budget or raises `ResponseLimitError`, and `enforce_response_limit(context, response)` makes repeated middleware enforcement idempotent.
