Kubernetes apimachinery cannot capture portable REST-mapping knowledge, compare discovery generations, or reconstruct equivalent mappers without live delegates. Add catalogs with matching live and offline behavior across default, aggregate, priority, lazy, first-hit, legacy, and context-aware mapping surfaces and lazy reset generations, without changing online consumers.

Keep all public catalog APIs in `k8s.io/apimachinery/pkg/api/meta`: `MappingCatalog`, `MappingCatalogEntry`, `CatalogPriorityRule`, `CatalogConflict`, `MappingCatalog.Canonical`, `CatalogRESTMapperWithContext`, `DefaultRESTMapper.CatalogWithContext`, `MultiRESTMapper.CatalogWithContext`, `PriorityRESTMapper.CatalogWithContext`, `SnapshotRESTMapper`, `SnapshotRESTMapperWithContext`, `MappingCatalogCodec`, `NewMappingCatalogCodec`, `MappingCatalogCodec.Encode`, `MappingCatalogCodec.Decode`, `ErrCatalogVersion`, `CatalogChange`, `MappingCatalogDiff`, `DiffMappingCatalogs`, `CatalogRESTMapper`, and `NewCatalogRESTMapper`.

Catalog every observable candidate, scope, singular name, conflict, delegate precedence rule, and priority-narrowing rule. Canonical schema identity ignores registration order. Aggregate capture removes exact duplicates, preserves conflicting candidates in traversal order, and publishes nothing after cancellation or delegate failure. Priority replay preserves narrowing after registration order changes.

Each successful lazy capture belongs to one initialization generation. Reset hides earlier generations. Failed or canceled aggregate lazy capture remains retryable without exposing failed-generation entries.

Make versioned encoding deterministic and decoding atomic. `NewMappingCatalogCodec()` returns a codec where `(c *MappingCatalogCodec) Encode(catalog)` and `(c *MappingCatalogCodec) Decode(data)` round-trip complete catalogs, including ambiguous mappings and offline singularization. `DiffMappingCatalogs(before, after)` reports semantic additions, removals, and mapping changes independently of ordering or bytes, including across resets.

`SnapshotRESTMapper(mapper)`, `SnapshotRESTMapperWithContext(ctx, mapper)`, and each `CatalogWithContext(ctx)` preserve cancellation and adapter boundaries.
