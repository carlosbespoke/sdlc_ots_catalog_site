The Databricks SDK cannot consistently report logical transfer progress across HTTP, DBFS, Files, workspace, concatenated streams, retries, and multipart operations. Report bytes delivered or durably accepted without changing results or unobserved behavior.

Give each observed transfer one identity, one start event before monotonic snapshots, and one terminal event. Final counts remain stable with SUCCEEDED = 'succeeded', FAILED = 'failed', or ABANDONED = 'abandoned'. Closing an unfinished transfer abandons it. `TransferOutcome` is an Enum. Count only returned bytes, including buffered partial reads, or acknowledged writes. Exclude unread prefetching and duplicates after retries, recovery, EOF, or close. DBFS reads report decoded bytes at remote offsets; chunked writes advance after successful blocks. Seeks reset position without adding delivery. Concatenated streams share one position, and a boundary-crossing read emits one combined snapshot. Repeated EOF and close preserve native behavior and reports.

Multipart progress advances deterministically through contiguous completed ranges, regardless of completion order, and finishes after all parts succeed.
