DNS, manual, and delegating resolvers must suspend according to their continuation models. Treat delegating target and proxy children atomically, rolling back or terminating if either cannot suspend.

Return a receipt only after every preceding or racing callback is classified as published or retained. Resume coalesces retained updates into one newer generation and atomically exposes complete resolver, balancer, connectivity, and picker state. Idle entry, closure, cancellation, and resolver replacement terminate sessions without reviving discarded resolution or transport state. Unsuspended channels retain existing resolver, picker, fail-fast, retry, interception, cancellation, and shutdown behavior.
