# Recurring contribution charge ownership

The contribution team wants monthly Stripe renewals to support contributor edits and overlapping charge jobs without duplicate payments. Extend the charge job, signed Stripe webhook and GraphQL order mutations to account for each due subscription period once.

Requirements:
- A due period produces one successful Stripe payment, one CONTRIBUTION credit/debit pair, one `order.processed` activity, and one advance of `chargeNumber` and `nextChargeDate`; another run with the same `START_DATE` does not charge again. Orders not yet due remain unchanged.
- Jobs may overlap after their leases expire. A former owner cannot make another charge, record a second payment, advance the schedule or emit another activity after takeover. Ending that job cannot release its successor's job-wide lock, including when a third process starts.
- A job may die after Stripe succeeds without receiving a response. A subsequent job or signed `payment_intent.succeeded` webhook accounts for that payment without another successful charge. Repeated webhooks and a concurrent job response do not duplicate accounting or activity. A successful intent unrelated to an order changes nothing.
- An acknowledged `updateOrder` amount change during payment retains the amount actually charged for the current period; the new amount applies at the next period, without leaving the order ERROR.
- An acknowledged `cancelOrder` keeps the order CANCELLED and the subscription inactive, accounts for any already-successful in-flight payment once (including the period's schedule advance and activity), and prevents later charges.
- Preserve GraphQL `order` lookup by legacy ID, including its `legacyId` and `status` fields.

Work in `/app`. Run the job from `/app` with `node_modules/.bin/babel-node --extensions .js,.ts cron/hourly/00-charge-recurring-contributions.js --orders <order-id>`; `START_DATE` selects the due cutoff. The web command is `node dist/index.js` from `/app`, serving `/graphql/v2` and signed `POST /webhooks/stripe`. Local services: PostgreSQL (`PG_URL=postgres://app:app@localhost/opencollective_test`) and Redis (`REDIS_URL=redis://localhost:6379`); start them with `sudo service postgresql start` and `sudo service redis-server start`. Support configurable claim and job-wide lease durations via `RECURRING_CHARGE_LEASE_SECONDS` and `RECURRING_JOB_LOCK_SECONDS`. Apply migrations with `npm run db:migrate` and build with `npm run build:server`. Stripe is supplied in the runtime environment, with idempotent PaymentIntent creation and retrieval; no internet is required.
