Ordering currently trusts the price and name supplied by the checkout client. Make Catalog authoritative for `POST /api/orders` without changing `CreateOrderRequest` or the successful response shape. Accept an order only when every product exists in Catalog and each requested unit price equals its latest acknowledged price. Persist Catalog's name and price, not the supplied name. On a mismatch or unknown product return HTTP 400 naming all offending product IDs; do not create any part of the order. Keep the existing order flow and existing unit suites working; draft orders, discounts, Basket, item deletion and WebApp are outside this change.

Catalog's `POST /api/catalog/items`, `PUT /api/catalog/items/{id}` (v2), and delegated v1 `PUT /api/catalog/items` must publish every committed price on `eshop_event_bus` under `ProductPriceChangedIntegrationEvent`, including created products. Each price event must expose `ProductId` (the item's integer ID), `NewPrice` (the committed decimal price), and a per-product `Version` starting at 1 for the creation event and incrementing by 1 for each committed price change, in commit order. Writes through separate Catalog instances must serialize per item; a broker outage must not lose acknowledged changes. Ordering must enforce a changed price within 5 seconds of Catalog acknowledging it, including rejection of the old price. If Catalog becomes unavailable for two minutes, checkout continues using the last acknowledged prices (including products created before the outage and ordered while Catalog was available), accepting those prices and rejecting older ones. A change acknowledged while RabbitMQ is unavailable for 60 seconds must be enforced within 60 seconds after it returns, even when Catalog is then stopped. After 20 concurrent updates through two Catalog processes, checkout without Catalog must accept only the final committed price. The final versioned price event after concurrent writes must describe the final committed price.
