`statemachine` cannot prioritize eligible external events from every producer. Give each StateChart one pluggable policy whose decisions apply independently to every later external submission. `EventPriorityPolicy` is a Protocol. It receives immutable `PriorityContext`; an explicit integer overrides it for one event. Lower `EventPriority` values run first.

Keep equal priorities in admission order across repeated names, delayed reconsideration, and concurrency. Admit and execute each event exactly once without queue corruption or loss. Delayed work must not become eligible early. External work must not precede eventless or internal processing. Callback internal work completes before equal-priority external submissions.

Keep one synchronous processing owner and reentrant first-result behavior. Asynchronous submissions each receive their own result or exception without stranded futures. Equivalent engines must honor reversed priorities while returning each submission's result.

Preserve delayed work's eligibility and stable tie identity during reconsideration. Invocation completion and errors inherit stable priority context without changing invoke identity or payload. Autoforwarded and child-targeted sends preserve producer priority context, addressability, and unreachable-target errors.

Declarative `priority` and `priorityexpr` are mutually exclusive integers; evaluate expressions at execution. Native JSON, YAML, SCXML-derived actions, the action model, and published schema must agree on fields, valid integers, and invalid expressions. Declarative and programmatic priorities must order identically. Reject contradictions and non-integers, including booleans, before target dispatch.

Without a policy or explicit priority, preserve existing public and declarative dispatch, callback, invocation, cancellation, and result behavior. Exceptions raised by a policy propagate unchanged; invalid values returned by a policy raise `InvalidEventPriority`. Both use the producer's normal error channel, reject only that submission before admission, and leave later processing usable.
