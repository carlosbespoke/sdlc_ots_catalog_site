`ormar` cannot currently inspect how model values become database values and return again. Add opt-in conversion tracing across queries and transactions without changing persistence behavior or exposing sensitive data.

Export `ConversionDirection`, `ConversionStatus`, `ConversionDescriptor`, `ConversionEvent`, `ConversionPolicy`, `ConversionReport`, `ConversionTrace`, `ConversionFailure`, and `TracedQueryResult` from public `ormar`. `ConversionDirection` is a `str` and an `Enum`: `BIND = 'bind'`, `RESULT = 'result'`. `ConversionStatus` is a `str` and an `Enum`: `SUCCEEDED = 'succeeded'`, `FAILED = 'failed'`, `NULL = 'null'`, `IRREVERSIBLE = 'irreversible'`. `ConversionTrace` is a `Protocol`, `ConversionFailure` an `Exception`, and `TracedQueryResult` `Generic`.

Record only conversions and executions begun during a trace. Isolate concurrent and nested traces, excluding inner events from outer reports. Traces follow executor acquisition through standalone, explicit, and nested transactions, completing after scope exit. Correlate ordered bind, execution, and result events with direction, phase, field, column or lookup origin, representation, dialect, status, and parentage.

Default policy records types and value presence, never raw values. Copied and inherited configurations keep independently replaceable policies and descriptors without shared trace state. Correlate encryption with underlying conversion without exposing secrets, plaintext, ciphertext, or reversible derivatives.

Report distinct UUID, encrypted, Boolean, Decimal-precision, and parser fallback descriptors, including the selected encoder or decoder category. UUID conversion reports paired non-null bind and result observations sharing the configured descriptor, plus explicit null observations, without changing values. Preserve null handling, validation, filtering, round trips, and untraced results. The public UUID field's `conversion_descriptor` reports the representation reaching SQLAlchemy.

Preserve aliased and related filter origins and field identity. Queryset rebuilding retains trace intent without mutating its source; evaluation returns the ordinary value and completed report, including empty results. Conversion and driver failures record one failed statement correlated with earlier conversions, omit parameters, and re-raise `ConversionFailure` with `phase`, `field`, `dialect`, and `event`, preserving the cause. Record structured parser failures before re-raising. Keep nested transaction reports isolated and outer work observable after savepoint rollback.
