The Parquet converter currently skips a block once it has a valid marker. Changing sort columns or shard sizing therefore leaves existing blocks in their old layout. Rewriting files at the same keys is unsafe: a querier can still hold an older bucket-index snapshot and an opened shard, while the previous release continues reading the legacy keys.

To close this out:
- In `/app`, re-convert an eligible existing block when its marker format or any of the configured sort columns, row-group size, row-group count or column limit differs, and not otherwise. Publish a complete immutable generation and record it in the converter marker and, within 30 seconds, the bucket index: a v3 marker and a `generation` object in both JSON records with `id`, `shards`, and the configured `sort_columns` (optionally preceded by `__name__`), `max_rows_per_row_group`, `num_row_groups` and `max_num_columns`; the index must pick up a published marker even when the converter cannot write the index itself and stops right after writing the marker. Put each new generation's objects, one `.labels.parquet` and one `.chunks.parquet` per shard, beneath their TSDB block under a prefix of your choosing. With a one-second conversion interval, an eligible block should appear in its new layout within 45 seconds of the change.
- For previous-release readers, preserve the legacy Parquet files, keep the legacy shard count in the marker's top-level `shards` and the index's `parquet.shards`, and keep `parquet.version` at 2 or above. A conversion interrupted before publication must leave the previously published data readable; a converter that has lost ring ownership must not publish its stale work.
- Both the Parquet-only querier and Parquet store-gateway must serve complete, correct series throughout a three-to-one shard change. Shard caches must not conflate different generations. A previous-release querier must continue serving correct series.
- While the compactor and converter run with `-compactor.cleanup-interval=5s`, a one-second conversion interval and other settings at their defaults, keep superseded and abandoned generation objects for at least 30 seconds after their last upload, and superseded ones also for at least 30 seconds after being superseded; reclaim them within 45 seconds after that. Remove every generation within 45 seconds when its TSDB block is hard-deleted. Never remove an active generation, including after a stalled converter resumes following another converter's takeover.

A six-series block at `/app/fixture/tenant-a` has a v2 marker and three legacy Parquet shards. Copy it into a writable filesystem bucket; `-compactor.block-ranges=1ms,24h` makes it eligible. Build from `/app` with `CGO_ENABLED=0 go build -mod=vendor -o /tmp/cortex ./cmd/cortex`.
