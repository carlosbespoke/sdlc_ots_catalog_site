The trainings service currently saves a booking and spends a credit before the trainer service confirms the hour. A failed confirmation leaves a charged training without a slot; retries and cancellations can double-count credits. Make scheduling, cancellation, and rescheduling consistent across trainings, trainer, and users. Deliver source changes in `/app` (Go workspace); keep the existing HTTP routes and gRPC methods and fields, allowing additive contract changes.

A successful booking has one visible training, one unavailable trainer hour and one spent user credit. An unavailable hour or insufficient credit leaves all three unchanged. Concurrent trainees competing for an hour across two instances of each service produce exactly one winner; all losers retain their balances. Repeating a successful schedule for the same user and hour, cancel, or approval must not duplicate any training, charge, refund or booking. A repeated cancel or approval answers 200 or a 4xx status and changes nothing. Cancellation releases its hour and refunds once under `training.CancelBalanceDelta`; a reschedule or approved move releases the old hour, books the new hour and does not change the balance. Users must support at-most-once balance adjustments keyed by an operation ID, including across replicas; the same ID with different arguments must not change the balance. Expose the key as an additive string `operation_id` field (protobuf field number 3) on the existing `users.UpdateTrainingBalanceRequest` gRPC message. Trainer must support owner-keyed booking and release by training ID across replicas: retrying an owner succeeds, a competing owner cannot take or release its hour. Expose the owner as an additive string `training_id` field (protobuf field number 2) on the existing `trainer.UpdateHourRequest` gRPC message.

A request interrupted by a service crash, unavailable users or trainer process, or unavailable Firestore must resolve to either the complete outcome or no change within 30 seconds of all services recovering, even across instance restarts.
